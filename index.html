01 — ABOUT USBTYA / 2026

From our village.
For our village.

BTYA is simply a group of young people from Bijoypur Tinali who want to spend some time and energy doing useful things for the village.

Sometimes that means cleaning a common place, helping with a programme, arranging a game, sharing information or just getting people together.
